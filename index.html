AgeLens

How old do I look?

Fit your face inside the outline, take a photo, and a small AI model on your phone estimates your age from your facial features.

🔒 Private by design

Everything happens on this phone. Your photo is never uploaded, never saved, and disappears when you leave the result screen. No account, no ads, no tracking.

How does it work?
  1. Find the face. A tiny neural network (Tiny Face Detector) locates your face in the camera image.
  2. Crop it. Only the face area is passed on.
  3. Estimate age. A small convolutional neural network (AgeGenderNet from the open-source face-api project, about 420 KB) looks at patterns such as skin texture, face shape, and proportions, and outputs a number.
  4. Average. The app checks your photo and its mirror image and averages the two guesses for a steadier result.
How accurate is it?

It's a guess, often off by 5 years or more. Lighting, glasses, makeup, facial hair, expression, and camera quality all change the result. Models like this are trained on public photo collections that don't represent every age group and ethnicity equally, so they can be less accurate for some people — especially children and older adults.

Use it for fun and for learning about AI — never to verify anyone's real age.
